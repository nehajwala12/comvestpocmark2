01What we heard

You didn't ask for another tool. You asked for this one to grow up.

Six points from the walkthrough of your current build shape everything that follows. Correct anything we have read wrongly.

The platformCopilot was the right call.

Enterprise security, permission inheritance and cost made Microsoft the base. Mach Two builds on that choice instead of working around it.

What worksGen one already produces.

The priorityStart with the IDL.

It is the most core deliverable, the easiest place to show value quickly and the one with the widest buy-in. A broader knowledge dashboard can wait.

One set of numbers

A figure is entered once and read everywhere.

When the chat summary, the workbook and the deck each compute their own version of a number, small differences creep in and someone has to catch them. In Mach Two each figure has a single address on the source sheet. The summary, the backup and the deck point to that address, so a correction made once flows to all three.

Proposed test

Across every back-cast IDL, zero figures that differ between the summary, the Excel backup and the deck.

05Inside your Microsoft

Built inside your walls, not next to them.

This is the line that rules out the tools you have already reviewed. Mach Two is installed in your own Microsoft 365 and Azure. There is one build, in your environment, and nothing new to log into.

Comvest's Microsoft 365 and Azure

One sign-in with your Entra ID. Permissions inherited from each deal's SharePoint site.

Where your team works

Copilot stays the front door and calls Mach Two for the deal. The workspace opens in the deal's Teams channel and SharePoint site. Excel and PowerPoint templates stay as they are.

Mach Two · in your Azure subscription

Room reader, your logic as a versioned service, drafter, trace store, and AI models in your own Azure under your Microsoft agreement. It writes only to the deal workspace.

What it reads

Deal folders where you keep data room files today, past IDLs for the back-cast, and later the deal record, read only.

Your sign-in

Your team uses its existing Microsoft accounts. Access follows the people already on each deal.

Your bill

Model usage runs in your Azure under your Microsoft agreement. The proof of concept adds no subscription to anything of ours.

Your copy of the build

The workspace, the services and the trace sit in your tenant, where your IT and security teams can see them.

Your rules

The industrial logic, the scorecard and the templates are Comvest's. We turn them into tested, versioned services.

Your analysts decide

Nothing circulates until a named analyst signs it. Mach Two drafts; it does not decide.

Your existing agents

The screening and legal agents keep working. Mach Two gives them a deal to work in rather than replacing them.

What we need from IT

Named here so nothing surprises anyone. Each is part of the set-up stage.

  • An Azure subscription, or a resource group in an existing one, for the Mach Two services.
  • A test SharePoint site and Teams channel for the proof of concept.
  • An app registration in Entra ID, approved through your normal review.
  • Read access to the folders of the past deals chosen for the back-cast, with their final IDLs.
  • The current IDL template, Excel backup template and scorecard, with the formatting rules your QA check applies.
06The proof of concept

One deliverable, proved on deals where you already know the answer.

We start where the answer is known. Mach Two builds IDLs for past deals without seeing the team's final versions, and we compare them figure by figure. Then it runs alongside the team on a live deal.

In scope
  • The deal workspace for the IDL, in a test site in your tenant.
  • Reading the data room folder, with the request list for missing items.
  • The Excel backup and the IDL deck in your templates, from your logic.
  • File, page and cell trace for every figure, and one set of numbers across all outputs.
  • Legal terms from your legal agent placed into the IDL where a draft agreement exists.
  • Review, comments and named sign-off on the workspace.
Not yet
  • A firm-wide knowledge dashboard or search across deal history.
  • The IDL update and the preliminary IC memo.
  • Writing back to the deal record.
  • Portfolio monitoring after close.
  • Changes to your credit logic. We engineer it; we do not rewrite it.
